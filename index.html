About the Catholic AI Project

A curated guide to Catholic teaching, public statements, and thoughtful resources on artificial intelligence.

The Catholic AI Project explores Catholic teaching about artificial intelligence and its implications for human dignity, work, education, communication, and the common good. The goal is to provide clear resources for people who want to understand and engage the evolving conversation around AI from a Catholic perspective.

The information for this website comes mostly from a search for "artificial intelligence" (exact match) in the website of the Vatican. I retrieved and reviewed all 108 documents returned by the search as of July the 30th, 2026.
